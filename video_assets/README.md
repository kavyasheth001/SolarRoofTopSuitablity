# SolarSight Video Assets

This directory contains assets and scripts to create a motion graphic video explaining the SolarSight project.

## Contents

- `script.md`: Detailed scene-by-scene breakdown with visuals, voice-over cues, and on-screen text.
- `voiceover.txt`: Full voice-over script synchronized with timestamps.
- `storyboard.txt`: Text-based storyboard describing each scene's visuals and animations.
- `diagrams/`: Folder containing Mermaid diagram source files:
  - `architecture.mmd`: System architecture diagram
  - `shadow_analysis.mmd`: Workflow of the shadow analysis process
  - `score_breakdown.mmd`: Breakdown of the solar suitability score calculation

## How to Use These Assets

1. **Render Diagrams**: Use the Mermaid Live Editor (https://mermaid.live) or a similar tool to convert the `.mmd` files into SVG or PNG images. These can be used as visual elements in your video.

2. **Voice-over**: Record the voice-over script in `voiceover.txt` using a clear, energetic voice. You may use tools like Audacity (free) or Adobe Audition for recording and editing.

3. **Video Assembly**: Use a video editing tool (e.g., Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or free alternatives like DaVinci Resolve or Blender) to assemble the video based on the storyboard and script.

4. **Assets Needed**: You will need to create or source:
   - 3D models of Mumbai buildings (or use simple geometric representations)
   - Icons and illustrations (can be designed to match the project's color scheme)
   - Background music (optional, low volume under voice-over)
   - Sound effects for transitions and interactions (optional)

## Project Color Scheme
- Primary Blue: #1e3a5f (used in headers)
- Success Green: #10b981 (for suitable scores and positive metrics)
- Warning Amber: #f59e0b (for moderate scores)
- Error Red: #ef4444 (for unsuitable scores)
- Background: White or light gray
- Text: Dark gray (#334155) or white on dark backgrounds

## Video Specifications (Suggested)
- Duration: 2 minutes 30 seconds
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or 3840x2160 (4K)
- Frame Rate: 30 fps
- Format: MP4 (H.264 codec) or MOV (ProRes for editing)

## Notes
- The assets provided are meant to guide the creation of the video. You may adapt the script, visuals, and timing as needed.
- For accurate 3D shadow animation, consider using specialized GIS or 3D animation software that can simulate sun position and shadows.
- The voice-over script includes timing cues; adjust pacing based on your recording speed.
